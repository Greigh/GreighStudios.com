## Overview

Lgenia is a modern corporate website for a pharmaceutical company focused on affordable, high-quality medications and global health impact. Greigh Studios shaped the experience as a polished marketing surface with deliberate motion and a clear content hierarchy.

## Problem

Healthcare brands often ship sites that feel either clinical-cold or templated. Lgenia needed a presence that communicated seriousness and innovation without losing warmth or clarity.

## Approach

- Next.js App Router with Tailwind for a maintainable design system
- Strong section rhythm: hero, impact, diseases, news, and team
- Motion used for presence and hierarchy, not decoration noise
- Static-export-friendly architecture for reliable hosting

## Outcome

A production-ready corporate site that presents Lgenia as a modern health innovator. The build doubles as a Greigh Studios case study in brand-led web engineering.

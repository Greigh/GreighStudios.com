## Overview

FiHaven is a Greigh Studios product focused on making personal finance tools feel calm, capable, and trustworthy. The product direction prioritizes clear information architecture, modern web performance, and interfaces that stay out of the way.

## Problem

Most consumer finance products either overwhelm with dashboards or oversimplify until the product feels shallow. FiHaven aims for a middle path: enough depth for real decisions, without the noise.

## Approach

- Product framing around everyday money workflows instead of generic “fintech” tropes
- Component-driven UI with a restrained visual system
- Iterative builds with Next.js for a fast, maintainable product surface

## Outcome

FiHaven continues as an in-house Greigh Studios product. Live URLs and deeper metrics will land here as the product ships publicly.
